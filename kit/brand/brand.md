# Sofa Money brand (for videos)

**What it is:** an app that scans the stuff in your home and shows what it could be worth, so you can sell
it. It also finds subscriptions you forgot about. Tagline used in videos: "Money from home. Without leaving the sofa."
Other lines that worked: "Scan your room. Sell your stuff." / "It's not hidden. It's your stuff." /
"Know what your stuff is worth. Before someone else does."

**Handles and links:** Instagram @sofamoney.app · the link in bio is https://sofamoney-test.netlify.app ·
Mohammed's own account @mwdonig. Sofa Money is App 01 of Project 307 (@project307am), but **never mention
Project 307 or 3:07/307 in Sofa Money videos.**

## Fonts (installed by `npm install`, loaded from /node_modules/@fontsource)
- **Space Grotesk 700**: the "Sofa Money" wordmark and big prices (€1,240).
- **Inter 500-900**: all other text. 900 for punch lines.
- **Anton**: tall poster-style punch words when a scene needs them.
- **Chakra Petch / JetBrains Mono**: HUD / scanner / timestamp text.

## The app on screen
- Always label it **DEMO** and amounts **Example** (e.g. a small "Example amounts · Demo app" line).
- Scan look: gold scan line or sweep, gold corner brackets around items, price tags as dark pills with a gold
  border: item name + "~€130" + "EXAMPLE".
- End card: app icon (brand/app-icon.png), "Sofa Money" in gold Space Grotesk, a two-line promise,
  a gold "LINK IN BIO" button, "@sofamoney.app", and the small "Demo app · Example amounts" line.
- App details if needed: mascot "Euro Eddie" (a coin, hype man), ranks called "Sofa ranks", Free + Gold plans.
