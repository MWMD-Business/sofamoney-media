## What Mohammed has said about style
- 17 Nov: "more cinematic, shorter, grabs more attention, uses viral hook formats that are trending."
- 17 Nov: follow viral videos, then add the Sofa Money twist (the Wants and Needs idea).
- 17 Nov: loves meme/edit sounds like **"Night Vision"** and the **"Nextel" walkie chirp**: "now make a video with them".
- 24 Nov: didn't like the Eagle One missions. Wants **short story videos**: something interesting happens, then the product.
- 24-25 Nov: realistic 3D, cinematic (never flat 2D). "Higher quality" = realistic 3D look + bigger sound.
- He loves **pop-up questions** before every build.
